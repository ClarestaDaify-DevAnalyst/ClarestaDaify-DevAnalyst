## 💫 About Me:

I am documenting my learning journey as a **Junior Data Analyst from Indonesia** with a strong interest in **data analysis, data cleaning, and data visualization**.

Data Analytics is the process of collecting, processing, and analyzing large volumes of data to identify patterns, trends, and relationships that can generate valuable insights for **business decision-making and forecasting**.

With Data Analytics, organizations can improve decision making, enhance customer experience, optimize operational processes, and increase overall efficiency through data-driven strategies.

In practice, data analytics involves the use of modern tools and technologies to transform raw data into meaningful information. I am currently sharing my learning progress and hands-on experience in data analytics as part of my commitment to continuously developing my skills in this field.
